# WO-23 — Re-scope the enemy sheets to 2×2 hero cells

## Goal

Move the four enemy vehicle slots from 4×4 @64 px to 2×2 @128 px, keep only axis-aligned
subjects, and record the dropped cells as owed. No image is generated by this order.

## Context

WO-11 proved the shape of a working sheet on `art-02`: four 128 px cells, one axis-aligned
subject per cell, flat magenta field, no scenery. At 4×4 @64 the same model produced seven
off-contract cells (side-on boats, banked cars, lane paint shrinking the subject). `art-03…06`
are still 4×4 @64 and would repeat that.

Each of these slots currently lists 16 cells, most of them `alt` poses of the same vehicle.
Two by two keeps the four most distinct silhouettes per slot; the rest are owed.

## Files (whitelist)

- `pipeline/registry/slots.ts`
- `docs/ART_BIBLE.md` (only to record the per-slot table and the owed cells)

## Steps

1. `art-03-enemy-ground-a` → `grid 2×2`, `finalSize 128`, cells exactly:
   `enforcer motorcycle`, `switchblade interceptor`, `road lord heavy`,
   `division sports coupe`.
2. `art-04-enemy-ground-b` → `grid 2×2`, `finalSize 128`, cells exactly:
   `hauler armoured truck`, `turret rig`, `ram bulldozer`, `missile trailer`.
3. `art-05-enemy-air` → `grid 2×2`, `finalSize 128`, cells exactly:
   `attack helicopter`, `gunship large`, `hunter drone`, `jet fighter`.
4. `art-06-enemy-sea` → `grid 2×2`, `finalSize 128`, cells exactly:
   `gunboat`, `speedboat hostile`, `hovercraft`, `torpedo boat`.
5. For every one of the four slots, rewrite `subject` to describe **four separate hostile
   vehicles, one per cell, every one axis-aligned nose-up, same scale**, and rewrite
   `emphasis` to forbid: rotation/banking, three-quarter or side-on views, wake, spray, smoke,
   exhaust plume, road markings and any water/terrain texture behind the subject. In
   particular `art-06`'s current wording — "at a very slight three-quarter angle so they read
   as floating" — must go: boats are drawn exactly like everything else, straight down.
   Keep each slot's own identity lines (`red nose lights on every hostile` for art-03,
   `heavy units must look slow and massive` for art-04, `rotors drawn as a crisp four-pixel
   cross` for art-05).
6. Do not touch `art-01`, `art-02`, `art-07` or any 4×4 slot's grid, size or cells.
7. `docs/ART_BIBLE.md` §5: add the four slots to the grid table as `2 × 2 @ 128 px`, and list
   the owed cells per slot (the `alt` poses and the ones dropped above) as work for later
   2×2 slots.
